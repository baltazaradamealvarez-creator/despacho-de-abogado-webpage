What to ask before a tax decision

In this insight

← Insights

Direct answer

A tax decision should consider your operations, available documents and applicable rules. Review its implications before carrying it out.

Describe the full transaction

Explain what you intend to do, who is involved and the business objective. Advice detached from context may overlook relevant matters.

Distinguish an option from a guarantee

Tax planning assesses alternatives within the legal framework. It does not guarantee savings or eliminate the risk of review by an authority.

Ask what documentation is needed

Contracts, records and background may affect the assessment. Confirm required documents and the scope of any written opinion.

Confirm current rules

Do not treat an old publication as instructions for a current transaction. Requirements, dates and criteria must be checked for your circumstances and period.
