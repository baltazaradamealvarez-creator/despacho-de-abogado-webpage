04 / Frequently asked questions

Before we speak

Does an audit examine every transaction?

Not necessarily. The nature and extent of procedures depend on the engagement type and applicable standards. The scope must be clearly understood before work begins.

Can you audit a company whose accounting is handled by another firm?

You can request an engagement assessment. The firm must consider independence requirements, information availability and the coordination needed with those responsible for the records.

Can the report be used by a bank or investor?

That depends on the recipient’s requirements. Share those requirements before commissioning the work so the required report type and the firm’s ability to undertake it can be confirmed.

Does an audit guarantee that every irregularity will be detected?

No. Every review has objectives, a scope and limitations. It should not be presented as an absolute guarantee that errors or irregularities are absent, or as a promise of a favorable opinion.

What does our business need to get started?

A description of the requirement, the period concerned and the recipient’s conditions. The appropriate financial information and supporting documentation can then be specified.
