04 / Frequently asked questions

Before we speak

Can you coordinate with our internal team?

The proposal can establish a division of responsibilities with your team. Before work starts, the engagement identifies who prepares information, who reviews it and who authorizes the relevant actions.

Are tax returns included?

Included obligations and tax returns must be expressly identified in the proposal. The scope depends on your business activities, tax position and requirements.

Which documents do we need to begin?

Your needs are reviewed first. Relevant information is then requested, which may include accounting records, reports and tax documents. Please do not send confidential documents through the initial contact form.

Can you review previous periods?

You can request an assessment of that requirement. Document availability and the number of periods involved determine whether a separate scope and proposal are needed.

How are fees determined?

Fees reflect the volume of activity, the obligations included and the condition of available information. The proposal should explain what the service covers and which additional tasks are quoted separately.
