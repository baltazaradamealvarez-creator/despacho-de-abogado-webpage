02 / Services

What the service includes

  • Defining the problem and the objective of the engagement.
  • Reviewing information relevant to the decision.
  • Identifying alternatives, risks and dependencies.
  • Proposing priorities and practical next steps.
  • Presenting conclusions to the people responsible for the business.

The scope depends on the need identified. It does not automatically include systems implementation, fundraising or a complete transformation program. Decisions with tax implications may require additional Tax Advisory.

03 / Our approach

How we work

  1. Define the decision. Agree the question the project should help answer.
  2. Review the situation. Identify available information, constraints and assumptions.
  3. Assess alternatives. Compare implications against the agreed criteria.
  4. Establish priorities. Present recommendations and next steps within the engagement scope.

04 / Frequently asked questions

Before we speak

Does the problem need to be fully defined before we contact you?

No. Describe the decision you face or the situation that concerns you. The initial conversation helps clarify the need and assess whether an engagement would be appropriate.

Does consulting include implementing recommendations?

Only where expressly agreed. A proposal should distinguish analysis, recommendations and implementation support, with responsibilities and deliverables for each contracted component.

Can you work with our existing information?

Available information is reviewed when the scope is defined. Where relevant data is missing, the limitations and information needed before drawing conclusions should be identified.

How long does a project take?

This depends on the question, information availability and the people involved. A schedule is defined in the proposal. No standard turnaround is offered without understanding the specific circumstances.

Does consulting guarantee higher profits?

No. The service provides information and analysis to support decisions. Results depend on execution, market conditions and other business factors that should be considered when evaluating recommendations.
